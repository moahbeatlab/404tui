404tui

Pads, patterns and slicing for the Roland SP-404MKII. In a terminal, on a phone, on a laptop — live on the unit's own project.

What it looks like

Tap a picture to see it large. The phone pictures are the web page (sp404 serve); the others are the terminal program and the replay editor.

What it does

Edit the unit live

The web app follows the project that is loaded on the unit. A tool you keep is written to the unit, which then reads the project again.

Pattern and pad tools

Fill, Euclid, echo, cycle, nudge, pitch and more, each with a trial you hear before you keep it, and one undo step.

Slice samples

Cut, reorder, roll and shuffle; slices go to pads with a pattern under them.

Record and replay

Record the unit's audio with an event log, replay it as a picture of the unit, and render a video.

Try it now

The demo runs entirely in your browser against a pretend SP-404MKII: pads, patterns, Motion, Punch and Morph. The Tools (pattern, slicer, pad) need the program itself: sp404 serve on your computer.
